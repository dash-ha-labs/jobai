# Ticket: jobai-global-job-search-research
Initiative: jobai-user-needs / Content Strategy

## Product Anchor
JobAI aims to solve genuine job seeker frustrations. Before writing content or expanding features, we need a rigorous, categorized understanding of what blocks candidates globally. 

## Scope: Research Only
Do NOT write articles or build features. Output pure, categorized research into the `research/` directory.

### Task A (Agent 1): US & EU Markets
- Focus on North America and Europe.
- Topics: ATS barriers, ghosting, algorithmic bias, EU labor laws vs US at-will employment impacts on hiring speed, remote vs RTO mandates, ageism, and entry-level credential inflation.
- Deliverable: `research/job-search-issues-us-eu.md`.

### Task B (Agent 2): Emerging Markets & Global Synthesis
- Focus on APAC, LatAm, Africa, and cross-border/remote hiring challenges.
- Topics: Localized networking norms, language barriers, visa/sponsorship friction, gig economy overlap.
- **Integration**: Review existing `research/` files in the repository. Synthesize this new global data with our prior findings.
- Deliverable: `research/job-search-issues-global.md`.

## Output Formatting (Both Agents)
Categorize findings strictly:
1. **Structural Barriers** (ATS, laws, geography)
2. **Behavioral Friction** (Ghosting, long interview loops)
3. **Candidate Experience/Emotional Toll** (Burnout, scams)
4. **Platform Opportunities** (How a tool like JobAI could theoretically help within scope - brief mapping only).
