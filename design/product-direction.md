# JobAI product direction — September 25, 2026

This revision replaces the pastel/mascot direction. It is informed by desk research and the project owner's repeated feedback; no applicant interviews or engagement lift are claimed.

## People and tasks

Applicants need to make their experience legible, create a usable document, tailor it to an opportunity, and retain control over claims. This spans first jobs, career changes, trades, logistics, creative work, service work and professional roles. Gender is not a design segmentation variable. Age and familiarity with tools vary; readable text and explicit actions should work across those differences.

## Evidence and interpretation

- [LinkedIn 2026 research](https://news.linkedin.com/2026/LinkedIn-Research-Talent-2026): nearly 80% report being unprepared to find a job. Inference: reduce uncertainty with a clear task and meaningful completion feedback. Do not turn anxiety into urgency, fake scores or invented success claims.
- [NN/G progressive disclosure](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/progressive-disclosure/): keep frequent tasks visible, reveal specialized features when needed. Application: import/create first, content editing before formatting, optional extension setup later.
- [W3C contrast minimum](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/contrast-minimum): at least 4.5:1 for ordinary text, 3:1 for large text. Application: strong ink, muted text still readable, 16px body/inputs and at least 14px supporting interface text. Document rendering is excluded because the CV has its own print typography.
- [FIGR](https://figr.design/) and [Notion](https://www.notion.com/) inspected visually: limited top-level navigation, strong type hierarchy, quiet surfaces, purposeful product demonstrations. These are visual references, not evidence of what job seekers prefer. Purple is excluded by request.

## System

White surfaces, ink #17212f, action blue #245bd7, muted text #526071, borders #d9e0e9. Restrained blue/cyan highlights can reveal changes. No pastel-green mascot, ornamental sage palette, motivational wizard introduction or document preview on first arrival. Navigation uses a single consistent top bar. Primary buttons and fields are easy to identify; page titles are practical. Forms have enough width to read complete sentences. Animation shows reading/rewrite/change states and respects reduced motion; it must never imply live AI when a demo is simulated.

## Validation

Check actual desktop/mobile rendering, keyboard access, meaningful controls and no horizontal overflow. Verify existing CV persistence, template/style controls, import review, PDF export hooks and unsaved-change protection. Keep earlier prototype records in the Round 2 archive. The current redesign has not been measured in an applicant study; do not declare a winner. A future round should use stable screens and a defined completion task before recruiting across occupations and ages.
